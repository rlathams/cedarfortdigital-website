Embedded Governance Intelligence

Start with plain English. End with executable governance.

Stronghold Enforce™ is the embedded governance intelligence and policy operationalization capability included with the core platform. It allows governance professionals to begin with plain English, Word documents, PDF source material, or policy-as-code and translate that governance into reviewed, validated, operational logic.

01 — Policy & enforcement

Policy and enforcement should not drift apart.

Plain English is a first-class starting point. Real Word and PDF source documents can be ingested directly. AI-mediated rule extraction proposes candidate rules, which move through a side-by-side review flow showing source policy on one side and generated code on the other. Syntax validation is built in.

Policy and control are stored as one object, reducing drift between documentation and execution.

02 — Why it matters

When governance stays trapped in documents, drift is inevitable.

Enforce turns governance into executable logic connected to live operations. Boundaries become clearer, decisions become easier to govern, and institutional intent becomes easier to preserve.

03 — Live governance intelligence

Built for live governance intelligence.

Enforce is not just a policy library. It also supports governed internal operations through patterns, temporal analysis, absence detection, AI model governance monitoring, concentration visibility for internal operations, regulatory change lag awareness, and governance completeness assessment. Vendor-specific intelligence belongs in Stronghold Vendor Sentinel™ when that module is licensed.

Pattern intelligence

Behavioral patterns, temporal analysis, and absence detection across governed internal operations.

AI model governance

Monitoring of AI model behavior against governance policy.

Concentration visibility

Visibility into concentration of risk and activity across internal operations.

Regulatory change lag

Awareness of where operational practice has drifted from updated policy.

Governance completeness assessment across the operating model.
