About Cedar Fort Digital · Launched October 2025

Built right from day one.

Cedar Fort Digital launched in October 2025 to ship the governance architecture that institutions actually need — not a generation-old GRC platform with AI bolted on. Stronghold reflects 25+ years of institutional practice from a team of senior practitioners, delivered as a clean architecture from day one. New company. Deep practice.

01 — The premise

A clearer premise for AI governance.

The premise is simple: AI does not repair bad architecture, bad data, weak policy, or broken decisioning. It amplifies them.

Stronghold exists to govern that reality with stronger boundaries, clearer evidence, and structured human judgment where it matters most. This is not governance as an afterthought. It is governance built into the system itself.

AI does not fix broken systems. It scales them.

02 — Transformation

Built for real transformation.

Stronghold reflects decades of practical experience with large-scale transformation, governance, operating-model design, and institutional adoption.

It is designed not only to strengthen governance, but to make governed work more workable for the professionals responsible for consequential decisions.

03 — The team

Team biographies.

Rachel Latham

Founder & CEO

Rachel brings more than 25 years of VP-level experience in software architecture, process engineering, and enterprise transformation at institutions including Citigroup Global Consumer Technology, JP Morgan Corporate Bank, Freddie Mac, Fidelity, Wells Fargo, Florida Blue, and Amgen, alongside telecommunications experience including participation in the Oxford GSM Committee on AUC/EIR security standards in the 1990s. She has firsthand experience taking a governance framework from working group to global adoption. Rachel is the sole architect of the Governance by Design™ framework, the Stronghold™ platform, and all seven provisional patent applications.

Chris Soskin

Chief Transformation Officer

Chris developed the first Agile Audit methodology at Deloitte — a market-defining innovation that transformed internal audit practice before being widely adopted across the profession. His expertise spans New York Life, Anthem, Toyota, and multiple financial services and technology engagements. At Cedar Fort Digital, Chris leads the consulting practice and Human Factor Framework™ implementation, helping organizations navigate the cultural and operational shifts required for accountable governance at scale.

Lex Banks

Data Analytics, Accessibility and AI Ethics

Lex is a federal IT audit and compliance professional with deep expertise in Section 508 accessibility compliance testing, WCAG audit services, AI ethics, and data analytics — bringing a practitioner’s perspective shaped by years of hands-on federal IT governance work and a master’s degree in data analytics. Lex advises on accessibility compliance, AI ethics, and data analytics for Stronghold™ software and deployments, and supports clients on any of these areas where she can assist.
