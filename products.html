How Stronghold deploys

Cloud-native. Deployable in weeks.

Timeline

8–12 weeks to live

Core platform deployable in 8–12 weeks against a defined first decision surface (one pattern, one sector). Subsequent surfaces deploy incrementally without re-architecting.

Architecture

Azure-native, fully managed

Single-tenant cloud deployment on Microsoft Azure. No on-prem footprint. WORM evidence vault, row-level security, Entra ID. No data leaves the customer’s tenant.

Sizing

Built for mid- and large-enterprise governance

Designed for institutions with 50+ third parties, multi-sector regulatory exposure, or AI/decision systems requiring continuous oversight. Pilots scoped to a single decision surface.
