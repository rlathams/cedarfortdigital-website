Foundational Architecture

The governed foundation beneath the workflow.

Stronghold Governance Core™ is the foundational architecture of the suite. It provides the immutable vault, governed state model, enterprise controls, and structural support that keep policy, evidence, workflow, and oversight connected.

01 — The foundation

Not the star. The layer the rest rests on.

Governance Core™ supports the full suite by maintaining governed workflow and evidence relationships, storing policy objects and version history, preserving packets and attested outputs, and supporting vendor records, telemetry-backed governance, and evidence-linked oversight.

This is the layer that makes the rest of the system trustworthy.

02 — What Core™ provides

Immutable by design.

Immutable vault

Sealed evidence, preserved packets, attested outputs — retained in archive-grade storage.

Governed state model

Policy objects, version history, and governed workflow relationships maintained across the suite.

Role-aware access

Boundaries that preserve who can see, act on, or approve governed work.

Enterprise controls

Structural trust across the suite — the foundation, not the star.

Telemetry-backed governance

Operational signal captured against policy and preserved alongside the governed record.

Evidence-linked oversight

Vendor records, packets, and attested outputs tied to the evidence they rest on.
